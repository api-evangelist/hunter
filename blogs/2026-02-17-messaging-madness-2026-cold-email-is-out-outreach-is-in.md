<img alt="Messaging Madness 2026: Cold Email Is Out, Outreach Is In" src="https://hunter.io/blog/content/images/2026/02/Gemini--What-it-means-and-how-to-win-with-cold-email-in-2026--7-.png" /><p>It&#x2019;s back.</p><p>Last year, we launched an experiment.&#xa0;</p><p>We wanted to see what happened when we pitted the best cold email writers against each other in a bracket-style tournament.&#xa0;</p><p>The result? Messaging Madness.</p><p>We saw thousands of votes and one undeniable truth: the &quot;spray and pray&quot; era is long-gone.</p><p>Welcome to Messaging Madness 2026.&#xa0;</p><p>This year, the scenarios are tougher, and the philosophy has changed.&#xa0;</p><p>Let&#x2019;s walk you through the competition where you can win $1,000 for writing <u>one</u> outreach email and how to enter.</p><h2 id="what-is-messaging-madness">What is Messaging Madness?</h2><p>Messaging Madness is an email-writing competition where 64 competitors are split into four scenarios. The goal is to write an outreach email based on one scenario.&#xa0;</p><p>From there, the public votes on the emails that they&#x2019;re most likely to reply to. Each round features a one-on-one matchup in which one entrant moves forward, and the other is eliminated based on the votes they receive.&#xa0;</p><p>The rounds are fast. The votes matter. And entrants can promote their emails however they see fit. Those who make the last four will receive a year of Hunter&#x2019;s Starter plan.</p><p>Win, and you&#x2019;ll earn $1,000!</p><h2 id="lead-gen-doesn%E2%80%99t-need-to-be-cold">Lead gen doesn&#x2019;t need to be cold</h2><p>In 2025, we called this a &quot;cold email&quot; competition. But looking back at the winning entries, we realized something important: None of them felt cold.</p><p>When you truly research your prospect, understand their specific pain points, and segment your list so effectively that your message lands at the exact right moment - that&#x2019;s not a &quot;cold email.&quot; That&#x2019;s outreach.</p><p>Cold implies you are a stranger intruding on their day. Outreach implies you are a peer offering a solution they&#x2019;ve been waiting for.</p><p>That is the theme of Messaging Madness 2026.&#xa0;</p><p>We aren&#x2019;t just looking for clever subject lines; we are looking for the vital connection between your recipient&#x2019;s pains and what you can do to help them turn a cold lead into a warm conversation.</p><h2 id="a-look-back-at-the-madness-of-2025">A look back at the Madness of 2025</h2><p>For those who missed it, last year was a whirlwind.&#xa0;</p><p>We reviewed hundreds of entries, narrowed them down to a final 64, and let the public vote.</p><p>We <a href="https://hunter.io/blog/what-we-learned-from-running-a-cold-email-competition/"><u>learned some incredible lessons</u></a> from the 2025 bracket:</p><ul><li>Relevance reigns supreme: The most &quot;clever&quot; emails often lose to the most <em>relevant</em> ones.</li><li>Length is a myth: We saw 200-word emails beat 50-word emails because the longer email actually said something of value.</li><li>The &quot;Human&quot; element: The winners didn&apos;t sound like AI templates. They sounded like humans writing to humans.</li></ul><h2 id="why-this-competition-exists">Why this competition exists</h2><p>We run Messaging Madness to prove a point: Writing a great email is only half the battle.</p><p>You can write the most poetic prose in the world, but if you send it to the wrong person, it&#x2019;s spam. Conversely, you can find the perfect lead, but if your message is generic, you&#x2019;ll be ignored.</p><p>To win in 2026, you need to master both:</p><ol><li>Segmentation: Knowing exactly who you are targeting and why.</li><li>Messaging: Articulating value that resonates with that specific segment.</li></ol><h2 id="the-rules-and-timeline">The rules and timeline</h2><p>This is a bracket-style tournament. Entering is simple, but writing the winning email is anything but easy.</p><ol><li>Choose your scenario: Pick one of the four scenarios below.</li><li>Write your outreach: Write the perfect initial email based on the prompt.</li><li>The selection: Our internal judges will select the top 16 entries for each scenario (64 total) to enter the bracket.</li><li>The vote: The public will vote on head-to-head matchups on the Hunter bracket until a champion is crowned.</li></ol><p>&#x1f4c5;  Entry deadline: March 10</p><p>&#x1f4e2; Bracket announcement: March 16</p><p>1&#xfe0f;&#x20e3;  First round voting: March 17-18</p><p>2&#xfe0f;&#x20e3;  Second round voting: March 19-23</p><p>&#x1f3c0;  Super 16 voting: March 24-25</p><p>&#x1f4d6;  Open-rate 8 voting: March 27-30</p><p>&#x1f525;  Follow-up 4 voting: March 31-April 3</p><p>&#x1f3c6;  Championship voting: April 6-7<br /></p><h2 id="the-2026-scenarios">The 2026 scenarios</h2><p>Each scenario is owned by a member of the Hunter team, who will act as the &quot;Commissioner&quot; for that bracket. These scenarios give you all you need to write the best email possible.</p><h3 id="scenario-1-the-founder">Scenario 1: The Founder</h3><p><em>Judge: James Milsom, Head of Marketing, Hunter</em></p><ul><li><strong>You are: </strong>A software Founder of an AI CRM start-up with agentic sales support.</li><li><strong>Your target: </strong>Chief Sales Officer (CSO) at a nationwide telecommunications company.</li><li><strong>The challenge: </strong>The CSO is under pressure to cut costs using AI, but their current chatbots are frustrating customers and tanking Net Promoter Scores (NPS). They are C-Suite level, looking for a solution that doesn&apos;t feel robotic.</li><li><strong>Your goal: </strong>Secure a meeting at C3 (the Customer Contact Conference) to share your analysis.</li><li><strong>Your offer: </strong>A 30-minute conversation at C3 to share a document identifying the exact moment their current bot fails, along with a fix.</li></ul><h3 id="scenario-2-the-content-manager">Scenario 2: The Content Manager</h3><p><em>Judge: Ziemek Bucko, Senior Content Manager, Hunter</em></p><ul><li><strong>You are: </strong>A Content Manager at a manufacturing firm.</li><li><strong>Your target: </strong>Communications Directors at podcasts like <em>Problem Solved</em>, <em>Well There&#x2019;s Your Problem</em>, and <em>Manufacturing Happy Hour</em>.</li><li><strong>The challenge: </strong>These podcasts are bombarded by guests. You need to stand out and make it a &quot;no-brainer&quot; for them to feature your Director of Engineering.</li><li><strong>Your goal: </strong>Book your Director of Engineering on the show to build relationships and support link building.</li><li><strong>Your offer: </strong>You will provide high-res schematics, a &apos;virtual tour&apos; video of your factory floor, and a technical case study PDF for their show notes.</li></ul><h3 id="scenario-3-the-ceo">Scenario 3: The CEO</h3><p><em>Judge: Matt Tharp, CEO, Hunter</em></p><ul><li><strong>You are: </strong>CEO of a recruitment company.</li><li><strong>Your target: </strong>Managing Partners in Commercial Real Estate (CRE).</li><li><strong>The challenge: </strong>Top producers are leaving for competitors with aggressive offers. They are stuck in a &quot;cost vs. resilience&quot; paradox.</li><li><strong>Your goal: </strong>Demonstrate value through exclusive data.</li><li><strong>Your offer: </strong>Exclusive access to peer benchmarks &amp; risk toolkits showing how competitors are investing in supply chains and which AI technologies actually help rather than adding cost. </li></ul><h2 id="how-can-you-enter-messaging-madness-2026">How can you enter Messaging Madness 2026?</h2><p>Do you have the skills to turn a cold lead into a warm opportunity? </p><p>Select your scenario, sharpen your copy, and submit your entry via the form below <strong><u>before the buzzer sounds for entries on March 10.</u></strong></p>
