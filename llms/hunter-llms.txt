# Hunter

> Hunter is an email finding and verification service. Its API finds professional email addresses
> associated with a company domain, verifies deliverability, enriches people and companies,
> discovers target accounts, and manages leads, lead lists and outreach sequences. Base URL is
> https://api.hunter.io/v2. Authentication is an API key passed as the api_key query parameter,
> the X-API-KEY header, or an Authorization: Bearer header.

Generated by API Evangelist from apis.yml and the artifacts in this repository. Hunter does not
publish an llms.txt of its own — https://hunter.io/llms.txt returned HTTP 404 on 2026-08-13.

## APIs

- [Domain Search](https://hunter.io/api-documentation/v2#domain-search): all email addresses at a domain, with confidence scores and sources.
- [Email Finder](https://hunter.io/api-documentation/v2#email-finder): the most likely address for a named person at a domain.
- [Email Verifier](https://hunter.io/api-documentation/v2#email-verifier): deliverability check for a single address.
- [Email Count](https://hunter.io/api-documentation/v2#email-count): free count of addresses known for a domain.
- [Discover](https://hunter.io/api-documentation/v2#discover): free company search by natural-language query or structured filters.
- [Email Enrichment](https://hunter.io/api-documentation/v2#email-enrichment): person data for an email address or LinkedIn profile.
- [Company Enrichment](https://hunter.io/api-documentation/v2#company-enrichment): firmographics for a domain.
- [Combined Enrichment](https://hunter.io/api-documentation/v2#combined-enrichment): person and company in one call.
- [Leads](https://hunter.io/api-documentation/v2#leads) and [Leads Lists](https://hunter.io/api-documentation/v2#leads_lists): stored prospect records.
- [Campaigns / Sequences](https://hunter.io/api-documentation/v2#campaigns): outreach sequences and recipients.
- [Account](https://hunter.io/api-documentation/v2#account): plan, credits and usage.
- [Logo API](https://logos.hunter.io/): unauthenticated company logo lookup by domain.

## Runtime semantics

- [Conventions](conventions/hunter-conventions.yml): auth, idempotency, pagination, error envelope, rate-limit signalling.
- Idempotency: the `Idempotency-Key` header is honoured on POST /v2/sequences only, with a 24-hour dedup window and body fingerprinting.
- Pagination: limit/offset with a meta block; Discover People uses a `search_after` cursor; max offset 10,000 and paid-plan gated.
- [Errors](errors/hunter-problem-types.yml): a proprietary `{"errors":[{"id","code","details"}]}` envelope, not RFC 9457. Note that 403 means rate-limited and 429 means out of credits — the inverse of the common convention.
- [Rate limits](rate-limits/hunter-rate-limits.yml): 15 req/s and 500 req/min on most endpoints, 10/s and 300/min on Email Verifier, 5/s and 50/min on Discover. No X-RateLimit response headers are published.
- [Sandbox](sandbox/hunter-sandbox.yml): the literal API key `test-api-key` returns a fixed dummy response on Domain Search, Email Finder and Email Verifier.
